Conversion tracking

The tags are already wired into the site and fire the moment you paste real IDs into config.php. Until then they stay dormant, so nothing breaks and nothing is double-counted. Create the three actions below and copy each label into its constant.

Two paths — pick one, never both

Google Tag Manager (GTM-N5LK7BDP) is installed sitewide, and the site also pushes each conversion to the dataLayer as ahg_registration and ahg_contact_click. So you can measure through either route:

Direct — keep GA_MEASUREMENT_ID and GOOGLE_ADS_ID filled in below, and add no GA4 or Ads conversion tags inside GTM. Already wired, nothing more to build. Use GTM only for other vendors.

Via GTM — blank those two constants and build the GA4 config and Ads conversion tags in GTM, triggered off the dataLayer events. More flexible; you then change tags in the GTM UI instead of re-uploading PHP.

Doing both fires two GA4 configs on every pageview and two conversion tags on every signup. Your conversion count and cost-per-registration would both be wrong by roughly double.

Policy risks worth knowing before you spend

Each of these can get an ad — or the whole account — disapproved. Two of them are live on your site today.

In-house finance wording

"No credit checks" and "blacklisted welcome" appear on financing.php. Google's financial-services policy treats that language as high risk and it can get a whole account suspended, not just an ad. Keep it off the ads and off /register-to-bid.php. If you want to advertise finance, build it as a separate campaign with its own landing page so a disapproval cannot take the auction campaign down with it.

Price claims must match the landing page

Google checks that a price or discount in an ad is findable on the page. Because stock turns over daily, none of the headlines above name a rand figure — they state the pricing basis ("at the bank's price", "below retail value") instead, which stays true.

"Repossessed" is fine, "stolen" and "impounded" are not

Keep the vocabulary to repossessed, liquidation, insolvency and bank stock. Those are accurate and policy-safe.

Scam-alert positioning

Your site already warns about people impersonating AHG. Run the brand group (D) permanently, even at low volume — it is the cheapest way to make sure the real site sits above any impersonator bidding on your name.

Why the articles get no traffic

You have 88 published articles and they bring nothing. It is not a content quality problem — your click-through rate is 18.2%, which is well above average. Google was never told the articles exist, and once a reader arrived there was nowhere to go. Every finding below is from your own Search Console or the live site, not an estimate.

  • Every one of your top queries is your own brand name.

    Confirmed in Search Console

    Search Console shows 1,660 clicks from 9,110 impressions over three months — an 18.2% click-through rate, which is strong. But the top ten queries are auction hub, auctionhub, auction hub sa, auctionhub sa, auction hub group and variants. Every one is someone typing your name because they already knew it. Nothing in the top ten is a person searching for a repossessed car who has never heard of you. That is what the ads and the article funnel are for.

  • The sitemap Google fetches lists 14 URLs. None of them are articles.

    Confirmed in Search Console

    Search Console reports /sitemap.xml as Success with 14 discovered pages, and /articles.xml as 1 error, 0 discovered pages. sitemap.xml on the live site is a stale static file; sitemap.php generates the real thing at 117 URLs including all 88 articles, but Apache serves the static file whenever it exists so the generator's output never reaches Google.

  • The live site has no vehicles in it at all.

    Blocker

    auctions.php on production reports "0 vehicles found", and sold_vehicles.php reports 0 too. The 524 and 590 figures quoted earlier in this project were from the local development database, not the live site — the production auctions table is empty. The sitemap is therefore correct to list no vehicle URLs. Nothing here is a code fault, but it is a hard blocker: do not switch on ad spend until real stock is loaded, because every rand would send a buyer to an empty listings page.

  • You have one external backlink. It is from TikTok.

    Confirmed in Search Console

    Search Console Links reports 1 external link against 4,860 internal ones. Backlinks are most of what decides whether you can rank for a competitive term like repossessed cars for sale. With one, you cannot — which is why the brand terms rank and nothing else does. This is the slow half of the work: dealer directories, the auctioneers' association, local press, and the banks you already work with.

  • news-sitemap.xml and feed.xml both return 404.

    Fixed by step 1

    robots.txt points Google at news-sitemap.xml, which 404s — that is the 1 error on articles.xml. The .htaccess rewrites that should serve them are not being applied on the production host. Running the generator writes them as real files, which sidesteps mod_rewrite entirely.

  • Every page shipped its brand name twice in the title tag.

    Fixed

    header.php appends "— Auction Hub Group" to every title, and eleven pages already included it. "Browse Auctions — Auction Hub Group — Auction Hub Group" spent 20 of the ~60 characters Google displays on a repeated brand. All eleven rewritten to lead with the terms buyers search.

  • Articles had no path into the auction funnel.

    Fixed

    An article ranked, was read, and dead-ended — no links to any vehicle, so no reason for Google to treat the listings as important and no reason for a reader to become a bidder. Every article now carries two strips of live lots, matched to the makes the article mentions.
